OFFICIAL GROUP PROFILE

Royal SMP Security Team

Independent organization

Founded by the citizens after the Great War to prevent and stop conflicts between kingdoms.

← Back to all groups
01

Founded

Founded by the citizens after the Great War because they wanted to help prevent another war between kingdoms.

02

Leadership

Leader: Jayme. Jayme personally chooses the members of the Security Team.

03

Independence

The Security Team is independent and does not report to another kingdom or organization.

04

Purpose

Its responsibility is to prevent and stop conflicts between kingdoms.

05

Authority

The team can enter any kingdom’s territory when responding to an inter-kingdom conflict.

06

Warnings and punishments

The Security Team can issue warnings and punishments to kingdoms that break the peace. These decisions are made by the team together.

07

Uniform

Plain diamond armor with no trim.

08

Headquarters

Spawn.

09

Rules

It follows almost the same rules as the rest of the Royal Crimson SMP.

10

Official banner

The official banner has a white-to-pink-to-red gradient and a golden-orange sun emblem. The sun represents royalty.
