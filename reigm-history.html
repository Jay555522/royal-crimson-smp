The History of Reigm Empire

01

The Founding of Reigm Empire

Reigm Empire was founded on September 3, 1966. It became the oldest kingdom in the Royal Crimson SMP. Its identity is built around peace, justice, and royal leadership.

02

King Eric and Queen Lizabeth

King Eric was a former ruler of Reigm Empire. He died during the Great War. His wife, Queen Lizabeth, was heartbroken by his death and passed away afterward.

03

The Great War

During the Great War, Reigm Empire joined forces with Zephyrial Empire against Endoria. Reigm and Zephyrial were eventually forced to retreat. The war ended without a winner.

04

Jackson and the Royal Army

Jackson served as Second-in-Command under King Eric. He died during the Great War. His son James later followed in his footsteps and became Royal Army Commander.

05

Lizzie's Story

Lizzie survived the Great War. She encouraged her brother James to continue their father's legacy by serving Reigm Empire. She lives elsewhere in the server, stays in touch with James, and sometimes visits the castle.

06

The Reign of King Legoman1927

King Legoman1927 is the current ruler of Reigm Empire. He is the son of King Eric and Queen Lizabeth. Under his leadership, the Empire continues its legacy of peace, justice, and royal tradition.
