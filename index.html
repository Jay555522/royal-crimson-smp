ABOUT THE COMMUNITY

A world with its own story.

The Royal Crimson SMP is a community built around kingdoms, leadership, alliances, and history.

Our world is home to different kingdoms and organizations, each with its own leadership, identity, and responsibilities. From royal governments to server-wide security, every group plays a role in shaping the world of the Royal Crimson SMP.

A LEGACY OF HISTORY

Every kingdom has a story.

The SMP’s history includes alliances, changing leadership, and the Great War. Reigm and Zephyrial allied against Endoria; both were forced to retreat, and there was no winner. The Royal SMP Security Team was founded by citizens afterward to help prevent future conflicts between kingdoms.
