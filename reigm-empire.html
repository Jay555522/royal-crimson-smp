OFFICIAL GROUP PROFILE

Reigm Empire

An Empire of Peace and Justice

The oldest kingdom in the Royal Crimson SMP.

← Back to all groups
01

Identity

An Empire of Peace and Justice.

02

History

Founded September 3, 1966. Reigm Empire is the oldest kingdom in the Royal Crimson SMP.

03

Leadership

Current ruler: King Legoman1927. Former ruler King Eric passed away during the Great War. Queen Lizabeth passed away after Eric.

04

Military

The Royal Army. Its commander is James, who also serves as Second-in-Command, Royal Advisor, and the King’s personal protector.

05

Royal family

Princess Jaymee is the King’s sister and lives at the castle without a governing role. James’s sister Lizzie lives elsewhere in the server, keeps in touch, and sometimes visits.

06

Territory

Sir Alex is in charge of the Royal Territory. When he attends Royal Council, he selects the strongest available Royal Army soldier as Acting Commander. The King must be informed and may reject the selection.

07

Great War

Reigm allied with Zephyrial against Endoria. Reigm and Zephyrial were forced to retreat; there was no winner.

08

Official flag

The official flag is the crimson-red banner with a dark emblem. Founding Day is celebrated September 3.

09

Royal Council and voting

Voting is open to eligible soldiers and civilians, with no age limit. The King does not cast a vote in this process. Candidates debate before another vote is held. In some votes, flyers are posted and, after two days, Royal Knights collect votes from houses. The King has a final hearing before the vote.

10

Royal Army training and recruitment

The Royal Army is for members aged 18 and older. The King personally selects recruits from the academy. Basic training takes place through the school, followed by a pledge for each new soldier. During the ceremony, the King taps the new soldier’s shoulders with the same sword the soldier receives. Promotions are based on performance and positive feedback.

11

Royal Army facilities

The Army has a training arena reserved for its members. It is used for training, knight practice, private competitions, and events. The Royal Army Headquarters and James’s command office are separate locations.

12

Royal Territory command procedure

When Sir Alex attends Royal Council, he chooses the strongest available Royal Army soldier to serve as Acting Commander of the Royal Territory. The King must be informed and may reject the choice. If the King rejects the selected soldier, Sir Alex must remain in the Royal Territory. Sir Alex may choose the same soldier again in the future, but makes a fresh selection based on who is strongest each time rather than relying on a permanent substitute. The Acting Commander controls the territory while Sir Alex is away.

13

Royal family and Great War history

James’s father served as Commander under King Eric and died during the Great War. James did not personally know King Eric. James’s sister Lizzie survived the war and encouraged James to follow their father’s footsteps into command. Queen Lizabeth, King Eric’s wife and the mother of King Legoman1927, passed away after Eric.
