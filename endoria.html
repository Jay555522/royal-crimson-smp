OFFICIAL GROUP PROFILE

Endoria — Realm of the Ender King

Security, Opportunity, Community, and a Chance to Build.

A kingdom with its main territory in The End and its capital in the Overworld.

← Back to all groups
01

Identity

Endoria — Realm of the Ender King.

02

History

Founded December 25, 1955, by King William.

03

Leadership

The current King is King William’s son; his name has not yet been confirmed. After King William passed away, his trusted knight led Endoria for one year. The trusted knight continues serving under the new King as a high-ranking knight.

04

Territory and capital

The main kingdom territory is The End. The capital is in the Overworld. The Royal Castle and Government Headquarters are located in The End.

05

Military

The Endorian Army. The new King chooses its permanent commander. An acting army leader also exists; the name and whether this is the trusted knight are not confirmed.

06

Royal Guard

The Royal Guard is made up of Endorian Army soldiers and has a separate leader, personally chosen by the new King.

07

Knights

Endoria has high-ranking and lower-ranking knights who help protect the King and Royal Castle. Lower-ranking knights wear diamond armor with purple trim. High-ranking knights wear netherite armor with purple trim.

08

Government and motto

The Royal Council includes all leaders, and all leaders have equal votes, including the ruler. Motto: “Security, Opportunity, Community, and a Chance to Build.”

09

Celebration

The annual celebration is called The Endoria Celebration. Its date has not yet been confirmed.

10

Great War

Endoria opposed the alliance between Reigm and Zephyrial. The other two empires were forced to retreat; there was no winner.
