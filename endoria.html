A KINGDOM'S HISTORY

Endoria and the Great War

The Great War brought Endoria into conflict with the alliance of Reigm and Zephyrial. The conflict remains an important part of the SMP's shared history.

A Chapter in the Server's History

Reigm and Zephyrial fought together against Endoria during the Great War. Both allied empires were eventually forced to retreat, and there was no winner.

The war changed relationships between the kingdoms and helped shape the history of the Royal Crimson SMP.

THE FUTURE

The Future of Endoria

With its history, royal bloodline, military, and values, Endoria continues to be an important part of the Royal Crimson SMP.
